Which door is the real exit?

This mirrors BB84-style exchange. Alice sends each qubit prepared in a random setup, and Bob guesses a setup. Matching setups keep the bit; mismatches are thrown away. The two doors stand for the two possible setups.

How to Play

ROOM 1

  1. Press Start round.
  2. Tap the correct door before time runs out.
  3. Collect 6 successful bits.

ROOM 2

  1. Inspect up to 5 cameras.
  2. Compare Alice’s and Bob’s bits.
  3. Choose SPY DETECTED or SYSTEM SAFE.
  4. Get 3 correct calls in a row to clear the room.
